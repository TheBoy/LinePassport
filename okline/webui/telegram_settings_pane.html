My Telegram

Telegram opens and shows a Start button. Press it to finish. Open Telegram
  • To answer a customer, press Reply on their alert and type. It is sent from your shop account, in your name.
  • "I'll take it" makes the chat yours; "Close chat" marks it done.
  • Type /stop in the bot to stop alerts.

Shop alert bot

Alerts come from a Telegram bot that belongs to your shop. Create one once; every team member links to it.
  1. In Telegram, open @BotFather and send /newbot.
  2. Choose a name and a username ending in "bot".
  3. Copy the token BotFather gives you and paste it below.
