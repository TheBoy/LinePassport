Customers

Know who each customer is, who is looking after them and what to do next.

Follow these steps

  1. 1
    Customers appear by themselves

    Anyone who chats one-to-one with any of your accounts gets a customer card. The same person on two accounts is one card.

  2. 2
    Take on a chat

    In Chat, open the customer panel and press "I'll take it", or just reply: the first person to reply looks after the chat.

  3. 3
    Tag, note and follow up

    Add tags like VIP, write notes the whole team can read, and set a follow-up with a date. You get a reminder when it is due.

  4. 4
    Save segments

    Filter the list, then save it as a segment to find the same group again.

Tips

  • A chat assigned to someone only notifies that person.
  • When a customer writes to a closed chat, it opens again by itself.

Questions

Why are groups missing?

Customer cards are for one-to-one chats. Group chats still work as usual in Chat.

The same person shows up twice

Tick both rows and choose "Merge into one". Chats, tags, notes and follow-ups move to one card.
